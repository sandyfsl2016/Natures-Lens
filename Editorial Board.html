Editorial Board

The Editorial Board, or (Editorial) Advisory Board, is a team of experts in the journal's field. Editorial Board members:

  • Review submitted manuscripts
  • Advise on journal policy and scope
  • Identify topics for special issues, which they may guest edit
  • Attract new authors and submissions
  • Promote the journal to their colleagues and peers
  • Assist the editors and reviewers in decision-making over issues such as plagiarism claims and submissions where reviewers can’t agree on a decision

Note From Editor in Chief

Dear Readers,

It is my privilege to welcome you to the latest issue of NatureLens: Forensic Science, a platform that bridges the disciplines of forensic science, law, and the pursuit of justice.

I am committed to fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and advancing research that strengthens the interface of science and law. Our mission remains steadfast: to provide cutting-edge insights, foster academic rigor, and contribute to evidence-based policymaking.

We explore groundbreaking advancements in forensic genomics, the ethical challenges of digital forensics, and new frameworks for legal standards in evidence evaluation. I am excited about the feature article on the integration of AI in forensic investigations, which opens new horizons for both science and law.

The rapid advancement of technology persistently confronts conventional techniques of forensic inquiry and legal proceedings. It is essential to retain scientific integrity while ensuring that progress adheres to legal and ethical standards during these transitions. I express my appreciation to our authors for their innovative contributions, our reviewers for their thorough assessments, and you, our readers, for your steadfast involvement with our work. I encourage scientists, researchers, practitioners, and academicians to continue contributing to this vibrant discourse. Together, we can ensure that the fields of forensic science and law evolve to meet the challenges of tomorrow.

Thank you for being part of our journey. I hope you find this issue both inspiring and thought-provoking.

Sincerely,

Dr. Rajesh Mishra (IPS)
