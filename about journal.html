About Nature Lens: The Forensic Science

ISSN: 07721:350221

Nature Lens: The Forensic Science is an international, multidisciplinary, peer-reviewed journal that focuses on various scientific fields related to forensic sciences. It publishes comprehensive reviews, standard research articles, case studies, and special issues on specific topics.

The purpose of forensic sciences is to motivate researchers to share their experimental and theoretical findings in as much detail as feasible. Consequently, the journal imposes no limitations on the maximum length of submitted papers. Complete experimental information must be included to ensure that the findings can be replicated.

Areas of Study

Areas of study encompass:

  • Forensic pathology and histochemistry
  • Chemistry, biochemistry, and toxicology
  • Biology, serology, odontology, psychiatry, and anthropology
  • Digital forensics, physical sciences, firearms, and document examination
  • Research relevant to public health and the intersection of science, medicine, and law

Journal Features

It operates as a hybrid journal with rigorous peer review and encompasses a wide international viewpoint. This journal serves as the official publication for the Faculty of Forensic and Legal Medicine of the State Forensic Science Laboratory, Raipur.

As part of the Journal Forward Series, this journal offers a streamlined, faster publication experience with a strong emphasis on integrity. Authors receive practical support to maximize the reach and discoverability of their work.

  • Rapid Communications
